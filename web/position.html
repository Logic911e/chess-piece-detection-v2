Photograph a board. Get the position.

Choose a photo of a board. The page finds the board, places every piece on its square and gives you a diagram and a FEN to open in an analysis board. If the board is not found, or the result looks off, you can place the four corners yourself.

Choose a photo of a chess position. JPEG or PNG; larger files are shrunk in your browser before they are sent.

Drag each handle to the matching corner of the 8x8 playing area, inside the board's frame. a1 is the corner at White's left. If the labels are on the wrong corners because you photographed from Black's side, use Turn labels. Arrow keys nudge a focused handle.

Before you start

  • Keep all four corners of the board in the photo.
  • Shoot from one side of the board and from as high as you comfortably can. A higher angle hides fewer pieces behind others.
  • The result is only as good as the corners. When the page finds them itself, check the yellow grid against the board and drag any corner that is off. A corner a little off the true one moves pieces onto the wrong squares.
